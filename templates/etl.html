Extract + Transform + Load
3-Stage ETL Pipeline Lifecycle
1. EXTRACT

Reads raw rows from operational OLTP tables: readers, books, and borrowing_transactions.

2. TRANSFORM

Handles NULL dates, cleans strings, standardizes categories, calculates borrowing_duration, computes overdue fines, and formats calendar keys.

3. LOAD

Maps surrogate keys and inserts/updates dim_reader, dim_book, dim_date, and fact_borrowing.
